Swimming

GUE requires a swim fitness test for all its levels of
certification. I strongly believe that divers should be able
to swim comfortably and reasonably efficiently. Sadly,
many divers are poor swimmers and rely on their gear
in the water to provide floatation and propulsion. This
is akin to relying on an equipment solution to what is
essentially a training issue.

Swimming is a very low impact sport, has a very high
calorie burn, and develops cardiovascular efficiency. I like
to say that swimmers are cardio machines and can remain
so their entire lives as it is such low impact. Swimming
is also inexpensive to get into: all you need are goggles,
a swimsuit, and access to an appropriate body of water.
