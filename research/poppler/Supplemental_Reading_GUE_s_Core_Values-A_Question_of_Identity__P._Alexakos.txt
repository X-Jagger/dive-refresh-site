Conservation
The fact that helium is a non-renewable resource,
especially one essential to applications that are
critical for human flourishing and well-being, should
provide us with ample justification to seek to conserve
it (as CCR obviously does). Therefore, in this regard
alone, it is plain that the conservation of helium
through CCR diving would support rather than
betray GUE’s core value to promote conservation.
However, this is not the only conservation value
possessed by CCRs. Because they do not introduce
damaging bubbles into fragile environments, they are
also singular tools in the human ability to document
the delicate and irreplaceable without doing harm.
Naturally, however, like all values, the value of
conservation is not absolute, and its pursuit must be
measured against other values that define GUE. But
if CCR diving can be conducted in a manner that
