MEASURING EXERCISE INTENSITY

it remains in the target range. As fitness increases, it will take a
greater effort to generate the same relative intensity (i.e., the same
percentage of maximal ability). For aerobic exercise, a simple
method to evaluate intensity in healthy individuals is based on
