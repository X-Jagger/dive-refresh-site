The Role of Training vs. R eal
Life Experience
Formal diver training is designed
to give aspiring divers the necessary
skills and knowledge to dive in
the “real world,” where they can
gradually build experience and skill.
To be sure, I am not suggesting that
divers should never conduct training
dives or skill refinement dives after
the completion of their training.
Rather, my point is that no matter
how robust the training or how
good the instructor, actual diving
experience in environments that
challenge one’s skills are necessary
for divers to expand their proficiency.
There is no substitute for real world
dives to become a capable diver; the
ocean is simply too big and the caves
