Stress

Try tensing up the arm (as the muscles tense up, there will be a
loss of mobility). Now relax and let the arm rest (the mobility
will return). The lesson is, if you are tense and having problems
doing a shutdown, leave it and come back to it when you are
feeling more relaxed rather than continue to struggle and get
more frustrated. So chill out, breathe slowly, and relax.

Trim

If a diver is horizontal in the water, the valves are going to be
where they are supposed to be. The further off horizontal a diver
is, the more likely the rig is to slip down the back a little, and
maybe just take those valves out of reach. So aim for flat trim.
